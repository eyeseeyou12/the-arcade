🎲 How to Play

This is a live shared game — you can open this any time, mid-turn, without affecting anyone else.

Goal: be the first to reach the final space (41) on the board.

Joining: pick a name and character. Up to 6 players are active on the board at once — anyone past that is placed in a live queue and auto-seated as slots open up.

Your turn: tap Take Turn, then choose a difficulty. Easy pays small and safe, Genius pays big but the wrong-answer penalty is bigger too. The exact +/- reward is always shown before you answer.

⚡ Force Cards: roughly 1 in 4 questions can be sent to a rival instead of answering it yourself — whoever ends up answering gets double the reward and double the penalty.

Board spaces:

Safe – can't be targeted by a Twist or Force Card until your own next turn.
Wormhole – if your very next question is wrong, you fall an extra 8 spaces.
Twist – next turn you can force your question onto a rival at fixed +10 / -3 odds.
Bonus – go again immediately.
Swap – trade board positions with a random rival.

Between rounds: once someone wins, the bottom half of that round's standings rotate to the back of the queue, and the rest reset and refill from the front of the queue for the next round.

Sharing: use the Game Code / Copy Invite Link button up top — anyone who opens that link joins this exact game.

Stuck? "Reset This Game" (top of the page) wipes this game back to an empty lobby for everyone — use it if a turn ever gets stuck. "New Game" spins up a brand new game code instead, leaving this one untouched.
