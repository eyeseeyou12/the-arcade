TYPE OR DIE
a typing-speed detonation challenge
HOW TO PLAY

> Words fall from the top of the lane. Type them before they hit the floor line.

> Every wrong key or missed word pushes the DETONATOR LEVER down.

> Type fast & clean (well above average WPM, zero mistakes) and the lever holds its position.

> Build a 25-word no-mistake streak and the lever rises a notch. Do it again at 50 and 75.

> Hit a full 100-word clean streak and the lever resets all the way to the top.

> If the lever falls all the way down... BOOM. Game over.
